Home/About HobbyBlox

The company behind the Blox

HobbyBlox designs and manufactures terrain pieces, Blox, that come together to create immersive worlds for hobbyists. One growing family of hobby brands, each built for the way its community plays.

Where it started

Born on a crawler course.

MountainBlox came first, built by a designer who was tired of spraying foam and painting cardboard every time he wanted a new indoor course. He wanted terrain that looked real, set up in minutes, and could be rearranged whenever the challenge got stale.

That idea became MountainBlox: realistic, portable RC crawler terrain that snaps together without tools. It also became a way of thinking about every hobby that lives on a landscape. RC crawling is better together, and so is building the world it happens in.

HobbyBlox is that idea, grown up. MountainBlox is available today. FingerBlox and DriftingBlox are in development. More Blox brands are on the drawing board.

What we make

Terrain. Not vehicles. Not venues.

Blox are the landscape your hobby happens on. Each Blox brand is engineered for its own hobby, some pieces stack and interlock, others are purpose-shaped forms, but every one is designed to be arranged, expanded and rebuilt.

Built to be arranged

Start with a kit, add Blox as you go. Change the layout whenever you want a new challenge.

Made for your hobby

Rock texture for crawlers. Concrete, coping and rails for fingerboards. Corners and camber for drift. Each brand speaks its hobby's language.

Made to last

Durable materials, precise molds and finishes that hold up to years of sessions, at home, in-store or on the road.

One growing ecosystem

Every brand makes the next one stronger.

Each HobbyBlox brand shares the same product development, manufacturing, marketing, e-commerce and dealer network, and the same community of builders, creators and venues. When one brand grows, the whole family benefits.

Meet our spirit guide

Squatch.

Freedom, strength and wild places. Squatch stands for the reason we crawl: getting out there, finding a line nobody else saw, and bringing friends along. Watch for him on packaging, layouts and at League events.

What scale is MountainBlox for?
Designed for 1/24 and 1/18 scale crawlers; 1/32 and 1/30 scale fit as well. 1/10 scale isn't supported yet, larger products are being considered.
Do I need tools or glue?
No. Blox snap together with connectors under each corner. Bridges hook on with L-hooks. Setup takes minutes, teardown is just as quick.
How do the magnets work?
Blox are molded with metallic powder, so magnetic gates, markers and accessories stick wherever you place them, no drilling.
Shipping & returns
Orders ship from the USA in 1-3 business days and typically arrive in 3-7. Unused items in original packaging can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. International shipping is coming.

Start here

From box to mountain in minutes.

Lay out your Blox row by row, add a connector under each corner before stacking or starting the next row, then hook on bridges and place your gates. No tools, no glue.

1 · Lay the baseStart on a flat floor or table and place the first row of Blox.
2 · ConnectSlide a connector under every corner before adding the next row.
3 · Stack & bridgeTunnel Junctions raise the course. Hook bridges on with L-hooks.
4 · Set gatesMagnetic gates stick anywhere. Pick a route and run it.

Build tips

Get more out of every Blox.

Stack for elevation

Put Tunnel Junctions under existing Blox or under each other. Every stack adds a climb on top and a route underneath.

Split the Faultline

Run the Split Faultline as one piece for a long crossing, or split it to squeeze a tight line into a corner of the room.

Rotate the difficulty

Change gate positions before changing Blox. A new route on the same layout is a new course.

Bridge with intent

Hook the Split Bridge between two summits for a high crossing, or drop one end for a steep descent.

Flat floor first

Blox lock best on a flat table or floor. Rugs and mats make connectors sit unevenly.

Photograph low

Camera at Blox level, backdrop behind, the micro-texture does the rest.
